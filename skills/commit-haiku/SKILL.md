When the user asks for a commit haiku or poem about their changes, use the `generate_commit_haiku` tool to analyze the current git diff and generate a haiku.

The haiku reflects the nature of the changes — additions, fixes, removals, or refactors — and follows a 5-7-5 syllable structure.

Example usage:
- "Generate a haiku for my changes"
- "What does commit-haiku say?"
- "Give me a poem about this commit"
- "Create a haiku about what I just changed"
